Leave Policy

Employees receive 24 paid leaves per calendar year.

Employees can carry forward a maximum of 10 unused leaves to the following year.

Sick leave exceeding three consecutive working days requires a medical certificate.

Employees should apply for planned leave at least 3 working days in advance.


Expense Policy

Employees can claim business travel expenses through the Expense Portal.

Hotel expenses are reimbursable up to INR 5,000 per night for domestic travel.

Meal expenses are reimbursable up to INR 1,000 per day.

Expense claims must be submitted within 15 days of completing the business trip.


IT Support

Employees should raise IT incidents through the Service Desk Portal.

For a production-critical incident, the employee should mark the priority as P1.

P1 incidents are monitored continuously by the Technology Support team.

For laptop-related issues, employees should contact the IT Helpdesk.
